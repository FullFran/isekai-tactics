Default sprite pack: cells taken from DawnLike v1.81
"DawnLike - 16x16 Universal Rogue-like tileset v1.81" by DragonDePlatino, palette (DB32) by DawnBringer.
Commissions sprites (Paladin, Warrior, Mage) commissioned by Romet.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/dawnlike-16x16-universal-rogue-like-tileset-v181
License: CC-BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ (author's README: DawnLike-README.txt)

Changes: 16x16 cells were cropped from the original sheets and rearranged into the three sheets
below; terrain cells composite an overlay (tree, hill, building) on a grass cell. No pixels redrawn.
Cell positions are (column, row) in 16 px units of the original files.

units.png and units-enemy.png (player / enemy look), one row per class, 6 columns:
  columns 0-1 = idle (Characters X0.png and X1.png, or Commissions walk frames 0 and 2),
  columns 2-5 = moving (X0, X1, X0, X1, or the Commissions front-facing walk row 0, frames 0-3).
